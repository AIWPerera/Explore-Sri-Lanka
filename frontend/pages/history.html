A JOURNEY THROUGH TIME

History of Sri Lanka

Discover the ancient kingdoms, magnificent monuments, colonial heritage and remarkable journey of our island.

OUR HERITAGE

A Legacy of Thousands of Years

Sri Lanka has a rich recorded history spanning more than 2,500 years. The island is celebrated for its ancient civilizations, Buddhist heritage, remarkable architecture and cultural traditions.

ANCIENT CIVILIZATIONS

Historic Kingdoms

377 BCE – 1017 CE

Anuradhapura Kingdom

One of Sri Lanka's earliest great capitals, renowned for its stupas, monasteries and advanced irrigation systems.

11th – 13th Century

Polonnaruwa Kingdom

A medieval capital famous for its stone sculptures, royal palaces, temples and impressive reservoirs.

15th Century – 1815

Kingdom of Kandy

The last independent Sinhala kingdom, known for its royal heritage and the Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic.

A CHANGING ISLAND

The Colonial Era

European colonial powers influenced Sri Lanka's trade, architecture, administration and society over several centuries.

Portuguese

1505 – 1658

Portuguese influence began along the coastal regions, affecting maritime trade, religion and local politics.

Dutch

1658 – 1796

Dutch rule left a lasting influence on coastal architecture, legal traditions and trade.

British

1796 – 1948

British rule brought island-wide colonial administration, railway development and plantation agriculture.

A NEW CHAPTER

Independence in 1948

On 4 February 1948, Ceylon gained independence from British rule. In 1972, the country became a republic and adopted the name Sri Lanka.
