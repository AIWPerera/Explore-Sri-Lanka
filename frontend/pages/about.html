WELCOME TO PARADISE

The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Sri Lanka is a tropical island nation located in South Asia. It is known for its ancient civilisations, diverse cultures, beautiful beaches, green mountains and remarkable wildlife.

The island has nine provinces and 25 districts. Each region has its own geography, history, communities, economic activities and attractions.
