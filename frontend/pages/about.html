About Sri Lanka

A land of breathtaking landscapes, ancient heritage, vibrant traditions, and unforgettable experiences.

WELCOME TO PARADISE

The Pearl of the Indian Ocean

Sri Lanka is a tropical island nation located in South Asia, surrounded by the beautiful Indian Ocean.

The country is famous for its ancient kingdoms, cultural heritage, golden beaches, lush tea plantations, wildlife, and warm hospitality.

From the historic cities of Anuradhapura and Polonnaruwa to the breathtaking hills of Ella and Nuwara Eliya, Sri Lanka offers something special for every visitor.
